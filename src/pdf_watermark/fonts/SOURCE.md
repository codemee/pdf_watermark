# Bundled font provenance

`NotoSansTC-Regular.ttf` is derived from Google Fonts' `NotoSansTC[wght].ttf` at commit `b950a7257470b900078f2bf3223823a8602de7e1`:

https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/b950a7257470b900078f2bf3223823a8602de7e1/ofl/notosanstc/NotoSansTC%5Bwght%5D.ttf

The variable font was pinned to its Regular weight without changing its glyph designs:

```text
fonttools varLib.instancer NotoSansTC-Variable.ttf wght=400 --update-name-table --output=NotoSansTC-Regular.ttf
```
